# Flags

Historical flags shown in the country panel and on the title card, as
256-pixel renders of files on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org),
each the realm's flag or banner in use about 1590 (found through Wikidata or by hand).
Realms not listed here have no file; the game draws them a flag from their
map colour (`src/ui/flag_art.gd`), and colonies fly their mother country's.
Each PNG keeps its source file's licence (those under CC BY-SA stay so).
